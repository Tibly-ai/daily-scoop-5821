Who We Are

Challenging the pace of modern dispatches.

Established 2021 • Brooklyn, NY

We founded The Daily Scoop on a simple premise: that neighborhood happenings and high cultural critique deserve the same editorial rigor.

In an era of automated feeds and instant reaction cycles, we choose to look closer. We walk the physical blocks of our communities, talk to the local printmakers, sit in the new community gardens, and spend hours in our Brooklyn archives. Our reporting is deliberate, slow, and thoroughly human.

As an independent publication, we answer exclusively to our readers and neighbors. Every dispatch, interview, and cultural digest is designed to celebrate the distinct details that make our collective spaces worth living in. We treat journalism not as a product, but as a shared civic record.

Our Pillars

How we print, assemble, and report.

01 / Local First

Deeply rooted community dispatches.

Every article begins in a neighborhood space. We attend municipal planning sessions, profile small storefronts, and capture the architectural updates of our historic districts. We believe local news is the bedrock of cultural health.

02 / Art Direction

A physical feel, even online.

Our layout draws inspiration from mid-century avant-garde pamphlets and artistic broadsheets. We work with local photographers to present visual stories that respect the reader's gaze and avoid frantic digital clutter.

Become a patron of community journalism.
